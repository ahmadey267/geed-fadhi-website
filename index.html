About us

About.

Founded in 2024 to bridge the gap between complex geopolitical realities and actionable policy solutions.

“A neutral, impartial, Somali-owned platform where ideas are tested, debates are structured and durable solutions are forged.”

Rooted in the Horn of Africa and serving Somali communities across Somalia, Somaliland, Djibouti, Ethiopia, Kenya and the wider region — and the global Somali diaspora — we are a trusted partner for governments, international organizations, civil society and the private sector.

We operate at the intersection of policy, politics and communications: political research, geopolitical analysis, communications strategy, media engagement, stakeholder management and institutional capacity building.

More than a consultancy, we are a platform. Somali challenges deserve Somali-led solutions — grounded in deep local knowledge and executed to world-class professional standards.
